Sales process features

Sales Process & Path

A Sales Process defines which Stage values a record type may use. Path renders those stages as a guided bar with per-stage key fields and guidance text — the highest-adoption feature in Sales Cloud for very little configuration effort.

Forecasting

Each stage maps to a Forecast Category (Pipeline, Best Case, Commit, Closed). Collaborative Forecasts roll amounts up the role hierarchy, so the hierarchy design directly determines whether the forecast is correct.

Opportunity Teams & Splits

Teams add collaborators with a role and access level. Opportunity Splits divide credit between them — revenue splits must total 100%, overlay splits need not.

Territory Management

Assigns Accounts to territories and users to territories, granting record access by geography or segment rather than by the org chart. Use it when the role hierarchy can't express who should see what.

Duplicate & data quality

Duplicate and matching rules, Account hierarchies, and Data.com-style enrichment keep the account base clean.

Einstein for Sales

Opportunity Scoring, Lead Scoring, Activity Capture and Conversation Insights — declarative AI that needs enough historical data to train. Worth knowing by name.

Entitlements & milestones (SLA management)

  • Entitlement — proof a customer is eligible for support, attached to an Account, Contact or Asset, with a start and end date.
  • Entitlement Process — the SLA definition, containing one or more milestones.
  • Milestone — a required step with a time target, e.g. "First Response within 1 hour", "Resolution within 24 hours".
  • Business Hours & Holidays — milestone clocks respect them, so a Friday-evening case isn't breached over the weekend.
  • Milestone Actions — Success, Warning and Violation actions (email alert, field update, task) drive escalation automatically.

Omni-Channel

Assignment rules (push to a queue)

  • Record lands in a queue and waits
  • Agents choose what to pick up — cherry-picking is possible
  • No visibility of agent capacity
  • Simple to configure
vs

Omni-Channel (push to an agent)

  • Routes to a specific available agent
  • Capacity model — each work item has a size, each agent a maximum
  • Routing by skills, priority and availability
  • Real-time supervisor view of queues and agent status
  • Works across cases, chats, calls and leads

Configuration pieces: Service Channel (which object) → Routing Configuration (priority, capacity model, routing model: Least Active / Most Available / External) → Queue or Skills → Presence Configuration and Presence Statuses → add the Omni-Channel widget to the console's utility bar.

Interview questions

1 Why can't I add a product to this Opportunity?Medium

Because the product has no PricebookEntry in the price book selected on the opportunity. The chain is: Product2 (the thing) → PricebookEntry (its price in a given book and currency) → OpportunityLineItem (it on this deal). Common causes: the product is inactive, it has no entry in the Standard price book (required before any custom book), the opportunity has no price book selected, or the currency doesn't match. In Apex tests, use Test.getStandardPricebookId().

2 What's the difference between Stage, Probability and Forecast Category?Medium

Stage is where the deal is in the sales process — the value reps update. Probability is a default percentage that auto-populates from the stage (editable per record) and drives Expected Revenue. Forecast Category (Pipeline, Best Case, Commit, Omitted, Closed) is what rolls into Collaborative Forecasts, also defaulted from the stage but independently changeable — so a rep can leave a deal in "Negotiation" while moving it to Commit.

3 When would you use Person Accounts, and what's the downside?Hard

For B2C, where your customer is an individual rather than a company — retail, banking, healthcare. A Person Account is a single record rendered from a linked Account and Contact.

Downsides: once enabled it cannot be turned off; it changes record-type and sharing behaviour; many AppExchange packages and integrations assume the standard model; and reporting becomes more complex because a Person Account appears in both Account and Contact contexts. Consider the "Individual" object or a standard B2B model with a personal contact first.

4 Assignment rules or Omni-Channel — how do you choose?Medium

Assignment rules push work into a queue and agents pull from it. Simple, declarative, no capacity awareness — and agents can cherry-pick the easy cases.

Omni-Channel pushes work to a specific available agent based on capacity, priority and skills, across cases, chats and calls, with a real-time supervisor view. Choose it when volume is high, work types are mixed, or SLAs depend on even distribution. Choose assignment rules for low-volume, single-channel support.

5 How do you implement "first response within 1 hour, resolution within 24"?Hard

Entitlements and milestones. Create an Entitlement Process with two milestones ("First Response" 60 minutes, "Resolution" 24 hours), attach the Entitlement to the Account/Contact/Asset, and set Business Hours so the clock pauses outside working hours and on holidays.

Then configure milestone actions: a Warning action at 75% to email the agent, a Violation action to escalate. Add an escalation rule as a safety net. Report on milestone completion for SLA compliance. Doing this with a scheduled flow instead is a common but inferior answer — it ignores business hours and has no native SLA reporting.

6 What is case deflection and how do you measure it?Medium

Answering a customer's question before a case is created — Knowledge search on an Experience Cloud site, an Einstein bot, or suggested articles in a case-submission form.

Measure it with the Case Deflection tracking on Experience Cloud (article viewed then no case created), Knowledge article view counts and ratings, and by trending case volume per channel against traffic. The business case is straightforward: a deflected case costs nothing, an agent-handled case costs an agent-hour.

7 A rep says the forecast is wrong. Where do you look?Hard

Forecasts roll up the role hierarchy, so start there: is the rep under the right manager? Then check the Forecast Category mapping on each stage (a stage mapped to Omitted silently disappears), the Close Date falling in the expected period, the fiscal year settings (custom fiscal years shift period boundaries), currency and dated exchange rates in multi-currency orgs, and whether Opportunity Splits are enabled — if so, the forecast uses the split amounts, not the full opportunity amount.

8 Lead vs Contact — when should a record be which?Easy

A Lead is an unqualified prospect where you don't yet know whether there's a real company or a real opportunity — it holds person and company data in one record precisely because that relationship is unconfirmed. A Contact is a known individual related to an Account.

Convert once the prospect is qualified. Some organisations skip Leads entirely and create Accounts and Contacts directly — valid when every enquiry comes from an existing customer, but you lose lead-source conversion reporting and the marketing funnel.

Quick check

1. An Opportunity's Amount field is greyed out. Why?
Once Opportunity Products exist, Amount is calculated from them. Remove the line items to edit it directly.
2. Which feature pauses the SLA clock outside working hours?
Business Hours and Holidays are respected natively by milestone clocks and escalation rules — a scheduled flow would have to reimplement all of that.
3. What does kav stand for in Knowledge__kav?
Each publish creates a new version; you almost always query the __kav object rather than the base article.
