Marketing Cloud & Account Engagement

The two marketing products, why they're completely different platforms, how each connects back to CRM, and the deliverability and consent rules that decide whether any of it works.

🧩
The key fact people get wrong Marketing Cloud Engagement is not built on the Salesforce platform. It's a separate acquisition with its own data model, its own login, its own languages (AMPscript, SSJS, SQL) and its own API. Account Engagement (formerly Pardot) is also separate, though far more tightly coupled to CRM. Neither uses Apex, SOQL or the sharing model.

The connector and sync behaviour The Salesforce connector syncs Prospects with Leads/Contacts on a schedule (near-real-time in modern versions). Understand which system wins on each field — the sync behaviour is configurable per field (Salesforce wins / Account Engagement wins / most recently updated). Getting this wrong overwrites sales-entered data with stale marketing data, which is the classic complaint.

The data model — Data Extensions

Marketing Cloud does not use Salesforce objects. Its tables are Data Extensions:

  • Standard DE — a plain table you define, with a primary key.
  • Sendable DE — has a subscriber relationship, so it can be emailed to.
  • Synchronized DE — a replicated copy of a Salesforce object, kept in sync by Marketing Cloud Connect.
  • Data Views — system tables holding send, open, click, bounce and unsubscribe history, queryable with SQL.
/* Automation Studio SQL Query activity — standard ANSI SQL over Data Extensions */
SELECT
    s.SubscriberKey,
    s.EmailAddress,
    o.EventDate AS LastOpen
FROM   Master_Subscribers  s
JOIN   _Open               o  ON o.SubscriberKey = s.SubscriberKey
WHERE  o.EventDate > DATEADD(day, -30, GETDATE())
  AND  s.Status = 'Active'

Marketing Cloud Connect

  • Links a Marketing Cloud account to one or more Salesforce orgs.
  • Enables Synchronized Data Extensions, sending to Salesforce Reports and Campaigns, and writing engagement activity back to CRM.
  • Requires a dedicated integration user in Salesforce with the right permissions.
  • Journey Builder can use a Salesforce Data entry source and take Salesforce actions (create a Task, update a record) mid-journey.

Deliverability & consent

📮
The most under-appreciated topic in marketing A technically perfect campaign that lands in spam has failed. Deliverability is an engineering concern, not a marketing one, and it comes up in interviews far more than people expect.

Technical setup

  • SPF, DKIM and DMARC records on your sending domain
  • A dedicated IP for high volume, warmed gradually
  • A branded sending domain rather than a shared one
  • Reply handling and bounce processing
  • Salesforce-side: Deliverability settings — set sandboxes to "System email only"

List hygiene & consent

  • Opt-in (explicit consent) — legally required in many jurisdictions
  • Honour unsubscribes immediately and globally
  • Suppress hard bounces and long-term non-openers
  • Never buy lists — spam traps destroy sender reputation
  • Salesforce: the Individual object holds consent; respect "Don't Market"
  • Preference centres beat all-or-nothing unsubscribe

Campaigns & attribution in CRM

  • Campaign and Campaign Member are the CRM-side record of who was targeted and how they responded.
  • Campaign Influence attributes Opportunity revenue back to campaigns — models include Primary Campaign Source, even distribution, and custom models.
  • Connected Campaigns (Account Engagement) keeps the two systems' campaigns aligned so influence reporting is meaningful.
  • The metric marketing is judged on is usually pipeline and revenue influenced, not email opens — build the reporting for that.

Interview questions

1 Account Engagement or Marketing Cloud — how do you advise a client?Medium

Ask about the motion, not the size. B2B with a long considered sales cycle, lead nurture and a hand-off to sales reps → Account Engagement: scoring and grading, Engagement Studio, tight Lead/Contact sync. B2C with millions of consumers across email, SMS, push and ads, needing 1:1 personalisation → Marketing Cloud Engagement.

Also flag the skills implication: Account Engagement is admin-style configuration; Marketing Cloud needs AMPscript, SQL and Data Extension modelling — a genuinely different hire.

2 Scoring vs grading — what's the difference?Easy

Scoring is numeric and behavioural: how interested are they, based on actions like page views, downloads and email clicks. Grading is a letter and profile-based: how well do they fit your ideal customer profile — industry, company size, job title.

You want both. A high score with a poor grade is an enthusiastic student who'll never buy; a high grade with a low score is a perfect-fit company that hasn't engaged yet. MQL thresholds should use both dimensions.

3 Is Marketing Cloud built on the Salesforce platform?Medium

No. Marketing Cloud Engagement is a separate acquisition with its own infrastructure, login, data model (Data Extensions, not sObjects), languages (AMPscript, SSJS, SQL) and REST/SOAP APIs. There is no Apex, no SOQL and no Salesforce sharing model inside it.

Integration is via Marketing Cloud Connect, which enables Synchronized Data Extensions and lets journeys read from and write back to CRM. Knowing this prevents a lot of bad architecture assumptions.

4 Sales complains marketing is overwriting their data. What's happening?Hard

The Account Engagement connector's per-field sync behaviour is set so that Account Engagement wins, or "most recently updated" wins and an automation is touching the prospect after the rep edits the record.

Fix by auditing field-level sync settings: for fields sales owns (phone, title corrections), set Salesforce wins. Also check automation rules that blanket-update prospects, and confirm the sync user's permissions. Agree a field-ownership matrix with both teams — this is a governance problem as much as a config one.

5 Emails are landing in spam. Where do you start?Hard

Technical authentication first: SPF, DKIM and DMARC correctly configured on the sending domain, a branded sending domain rather than shared, and — for high volume — a dedicated IP that has been warmed gradually rather than blasted from day one.

Then reputation: are you suppressing hard bounces and long-term non-openers? Was any list purchased (spam traps are fatal)? Is the unsubscribe honoured immediately? Are complaint rates elevated? Check the deliverability dashboard and seed-list testing. In Salesforce specifically, confirm sandbox Deliverability is set to "System email only" so test sends never hit real inboxes.

6 What is a Data Extension and how does it differ from a Salesforce object?Medium

A Data Extension is Marketing Cloud's table — you define its columns and primary key. It has no page layouts, no sharing model, no triggers and no SOQL; you query it with ANSI SQL in Automation Studio.

Variants: Sendable (has a subscriber relationship so it can be emailed), Synchronized (a replicated Salesforce object via Marketing Cloud Connect), and Data Views — system tables of send, open, click, bounce and unsubscribe history that you join against for engagement reporting.

7 How do you prove marketing generated revenue?Medium

Campaign Influence in CRM. Campaign Members record who was touched; influence models attribute Opportunity revenue back to campaigns — Primary Campaign Source, even distribution across all influencing campaigns, or a custom model weighting first-touch and last-touch.

For Account Engagement, enable Connected Campaigns so marketing and CRM campaigns are the same records — otherwise attribution silently breaks. Then report on pipeline and closed-won revenue influenced, which is the number the business actually cares about, rather than opens and clicks.

Quick check

1. Which product would you choose for a B2B software company nurturing 40,000 leads?
B2B nurture with scoring, grading and a hand-off to sales is exactly Account Engagement's design point — and its Lead/Contact sync is far tighter.
2. What language personalises content inside a Marketing Cloud email?
Marketing Cloud is a separate platform — no Apex, no SOQL. Personalisation is AMPscript/SSJS, and data manipulation is SQL over Data Extensions.
3. Which three DNS records underpin email deliverability?
They prove the sender is authorised, that the message wasn't tampered with, and tell receivers what to do when a check fails.
